We are creating a new website for Luxora Draws. The brand will initially focus on smaller prize bundles and cash competitions, including tech bundles, kitchen appliances, beauty products and lifestyle packages. Over time, the brand will evolve into higher value prizes such as luxury watches, premium items and eventually cars.

The website must feel premium, clean and aspirational from day one. The goal is to stand apart from typical competition websites, which often feel busy, bright and gimmicky.

The brand should feel:

Luxury

Minimal

Modern

Confident

High end

Not chaotic or tacky

It must feel established, even if we are starting small.

Target Audience

The platform is open to everyone aged 18+, however:

Naturally leans slightly male due to tech, watches and car aspirations

Beauty and lifestyle bundles should attract younger female audiences

Overall positioning should feel inclusive but elevated

We are not building a “lads only” brand. It must feel premium and universal.

Brand Direction

Colour Palette

These should reflect:

Luxury

Depth

Sophistication

Clean contrast

Avoid overly bright neon palettes.

Avoid loud red, orange or cluttered multi colour combinations.

Accent colours can be used subtly for urgency elements such as countdown timers and ticket indicators, but must remain refined.

Typography

Elegant serif font for headings, similar to the luxury watch reference provided

Clean modern sans serif for body text

Strong visual hierarchy

High end spacing and breathing room

We like the “big luxury header” style seen in the watch example.

Visual Style References

The client likes:

Large cinematic header image or video

Strong hero section with bold elegant typography

Refined countdown layout

Clean ticket information display

Product grid layout similar to the plant shop example

Structured spacing and balanced alignment

Premium photography with depth and shadows

The client does not like:

Plain white content boxes

Overly bright, busy designs

Generic competition website look

Cluttered layouts

Cheap looking badge graphics

Product / Competition Page Layout

The client likes:

Clean countdown placement

Clear ticket allocation display

Minimal clutter

Structured spacing

Avoid:

Heavy white card backgrounds

Overloaded statistics

Distracting badges

Ticket selectors should feel premium, not playful.

Cash alternative must be clearly displayed but subtle, not screaming.

Future Scalability

The design must support evolution into:

Watches

Higher value luxury goods

Cars

Meaning the aesthetic must already feel capable of holding high end prizes.

Offers & Incentives

We will offer:

Cash alternatives

Discount bundles

Early bird incentives

These must be designed elegantly.

No loud flashing sale banners.

Urgency should feel refined.

Overall Design Goals

The website should:

Feel clean

Feel expensive

Feel confident

Avoid chaos

Avoid gimmicks

Be memorable but not loud

We want something different from standard competition websites, while still converting effectively.

Luxury meets minimal, with subtle impact.
